Owner & Operator modes

The app opens in Operator Mode — safe to leave open on the shop-floor PC or a shared phone. Your operators can log production and manage inserts, but they can never see your costs or change past records.

Tap the OPERATOR chip in the top bar and enter your PIN to unlock Owner Mode. It locks itself again after 15 minutes of inactivity, or when the browser closes, or when you tap the chip.

04

Tooling stations

Each cutting tool on the job gets a station. A typical turned part might have three: OD Turning (CNMG insert), Grooving (MGMN insert), Drilling (solid carbide drill).

For each station you tell the app once:

  • Insert designation — e.g. CNMG 12 04 08, or pick it straight from your Inventory
  • Corners per insert — e.g. 4 cutting edges on a CNMG
  • Cost per insert — e.g. ₹400 (auto-filled if picked from Inventory)
  • For milling: inserts in the cutter — e.g. 5 inserts in a face mill
Tip: if the item is linked to Inventory, every insert change automatically reduces your stock count — and warns you before you run out.

06

Insert life & corners

Open any station to see its full insert history: every set loaded, every corner used, how many pieces each corner made, and who loaded what, when.

On the phone, the station screen gives you three big buttons:

  • ↻ INDEX CORNER — rotated the insert to a fresh edge? One tap records it.
  • REPLACE INSERTS — old set finished. Stock is deducted if linked to Inventory.
  • + LOG PRODUCTION — jumps straight to the log form.

The app learns your average corner life and warns when the current edge is past due — and it flags anomalies: a corner that died at 35 pieces when your average is 105 usually means a material problem, a speed/feed problem, or a bad insert batch. You'll see it flagged the same day, not at month end.

Anomaly example: "HSG-4020 · OD Turning — corner broke at 35 pcs (avg 105)". That's the app catching ₹400 worth of insert dying at one-third of its life — and telling you before it happens ten more times.

08

CPC — your real cost per component

CPC = insert money spent ÷ pieces produced. Not an estimate — calculated from the actual sets you loaded and the actual pieces logged. Owner-only; operators never see it.

Worked example — OD Turning station
Insert: CNMG 12 04 08, 4 corners₹400 / insert
Set fully used (all 4 corners)₹400 spent
Pieces made on that set420 pcs
Confirmed CPC₹400 ÷ 420 = ₹0.95 / pc

Two figures are shown: Confirmed CPC (only fully-spent sets — your bankable number) and Running CPC (includes the live set, proportional to corners used). For multi-op jobs, the total CPC divides all operations' tooling cost by the good pieces that cleared the final operation.

The CPC Compare tab ranks every insert you've ever run, cheapest per piece first — the honest way to compare a ₹280 insert against a ₹400 one.

11

Inventory

Track every insert, drill, end mill, holder and adaptor you own — designation, brand, grade, purchase price, quantity in stock.

  • Add items one by one, or paste a whole table from Excel — the app recognises columns like Product, Brand, Price, Qty automatically.
  • Link stations to inventory — picking an insert from stock auto-fills its designation and price, and every insert change deducts stock.
  • Low-stock warnings appear before a load would empty the drawer, and on the owner dashboard.
12

Reports & export

  • Owner Report — a printable A4 machining-efficiency report per job: stations, sets used, corner life, CPC, anomalies, recent log. Print it, sign it, file it.
  • Excel export — one click downloads your entire data as a spreadsheet that opens in Excel or LibreOffice.
  • JSON backup / restore — full data backup you can keep on a pen drive.
13

Sync & offline behaviour

All data lives on your device first — the app never needs internet to work. When a connection exists:

  • Every production entry, insert change, edit and delete is pushed to your cloud sheet within seconds.
  • If offline, changes queue and retry automatically — including after the browser restarts.
  • The dashboard's Cloud Backup panel and the Shift Proof chip always show the current state: Backed up / Waiting / Offline — queued.

15

FAQ

I forgot my Owner PIN. Contact TOOLFLUX on WhatsApp — we'll walk you through a reset. Your data is safe.

Can two machines share one app? Yes — log entries record which machine ran the parts. For fully separate data per PC, just open the link on each PC; each keeps its own records.

My operator logged a wrong quantity. Unlock Owner Mode, open the job's production log, tap the pencil on the entry, correct it.

Does clearing browser history delete my data? Clearing site data does. Take an Excel export or JSON backup periodically — and remember the cloud sheet holds everything that synced.

Will I get updates? Yes, automatically — refresh the page and you're on the latest version.
