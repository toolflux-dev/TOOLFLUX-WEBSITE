      Questions

      Which files can I send? A PDF of the drawing, or a clear, straight photo of the paper. Add the DXF or DWG too if you have it. We read dimensions from the drawing, so a CAD file on its own is not enough.

      Will it open in Mastercam? Yes. STEP is the standard way to move solids between CAD and CAM. In Mastercam use File, Open and choose STEP as the file type. The same file opens in SolidWorks, Fusion 360, NX and most CAM software.

      Which size is modelled when there is a tolerance? The nominal size, as drawn. If you want mid-tolerance sizes for programming, say so in the notes and we will model them that way.

      What happens to threads? Threaded holes are modelled at the tap-drill size with the thread noted in the email, because thread data does not travel inside a STEP file.

      What if the drawing is unclear? We email you a question before we model. A missing dimension is never guessed.

      How long does it take? Orders are modelled in the order they are paid, Monday to Saturday. If a job is waiting on the machine, WhatsApp us your order number.

      Who sees my drawing? Only us. It is kept in our own storage to make your model and is not shared or reused. Ask and we delete it.

      What if you cannot model it? If the part turns out to be outside what ₹79 covers, we tell you before starting and refund the ₹79 if you do not want a quote.

      Do I get a bill? Razorpay emails a payment receipt, and our confirmation email carries your order number.

      Can I send more than one part? Place one order per part, or send a batch on WhatsApp for a quote.
