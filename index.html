[04] — Services

Beyond the tool.
Total machining partnership.

TOOLFLUX doesn't just supply tools. We embed into your production process to optimise cycle times, reduce scrap rates and extend tool life through data-driven interventions.

001 Cutting Process Optimisation Engineering
+

Our applications engineers analyse your existing CNC/VMC programs, speeds, feeds and depth-of-cut parameters to identify cycle time savings and tool life improvements. Average cycle time reduction: 22%.

002 Toolpath & CAM Consultation Digital
+

Work directly with our CAM specialists to select optimal tooling strategies — from trochoidal milling to high-efficiency roughing (HER) — matched to your machine's dynamic capability.

003 Custom Tool Design & Manufacture Manufacturing
+

Special profiles, non-standard geometries, form tools and step drills manufactured to your drawing. Lead times from 10 working days. Full GD&T inspection reports supplied with every batch.

004 Tool Regrinding & Reconditioning Sustainability
+

Extend the life of premium solid carbide tools through our certified regrinding service. Re-coated, re-inspected, and returned to OEM-equivalent specification at up to 60% cost savings.

005 Inventory & VMI Management Supply Chain
+

Vendor-managed inventory (VMI) programmes to eliminate stock-outs at your tool crib. Auto-replenishment via barcode or RFID scanning. Dedicated account manager and monthly consumption analytics.

006 On-Site Training & Workshops Knowledge
+

Full-day machining workshops for your operators and programmers. Hands-on CNC training covering tool selection, offset setting, insert indexing and wear recognition. Certificates issued on completion.

007 Full Turnkey Tooling Solution Complete Service
+

A single TOOLFLUX partner handles everything: tool selection, setup sheets, offsets, first-off inspection, production release and ongoing tool management. Reduce your tooling complexity to zero.

Our Pune Innovation Centre partners with IITs, material scientists and machine tool builders to develop the next generation of cutting solutions for Industry 4.0 environments.

A01
Smart Tool Monitoring
Embedded vibration and temperature sensors in boring bars transmit real-time wear data to your CNC controller. Auto-offset adjustment eliminates manual gauging.
A02
Nano-Composite Coatings
AlTiN/Si3N4 nano-multilayer coatings developed in-house deliver 40% longer tool life in titanium and Inconel machining versus standard PVD grades.
A03
AI-Driven Tool Selection
Feed your material, machine specs and component drawing into TOOLFLUX.AI and receive a ranked tooling recommendation with cycle time and cost prediction.
A04
Additive Hybrid Tooling
3D-printed titanium tool bodies with brazed carbide tips. Custom internal coolant channels impossible with conventional manufacturing. 60% weight reduction.
A05
Minimum Quantity Lubrication
Near-dry machining solutions co-developed with leading MQL system manufacturers. Reduce coolant costs by 90% while improving chip evacuation in aluminium and cast iron.
A06
Digital Twin Validation
Every new tool geometry is validated in a physics-based machining simulator before cutting a single test piece. Reduces development cycle from 16 weeks to 3.
