      How the parser reads your statement

      Every line is matched on a leading date, then the money columns are read off the row. The direction of each transaction is decided by how the running balance moved, not by column position — that is what makes it work across IDFC and SBI layouts without per-bank tuning.

      After parsing, the whole statement is chain-checked: previous balance + received − paid = balance for every row. Any row that breaks the chain is flagged in the review table so you can see it before importing.

      Re-dropping the same statement is safe — duplicate rows are detected on date, narration, amount and balance, and skipped.
