01Who We Are

TOOLFLUX is a precision cutting-tool supply and application engineering business registered as a Sole Proprietorship under Indian law and operating from Hubli, Karnataka, India. Our address is 54, Laxmi Colony, Vidyanagar, Hubli 580021, Karnataka. GST Registration No.: 29EQAPD9045L1ZH.

We supply ISO carbide inserts, end mills, carbide drills, indexable toolholders, and related machining consumables to CNC and VMC manufacturers across India. We also provide on-site application engineering and parameter optimisation support.

This Privacy Policy describes what personal data TOOLFLUX collects when you use our website, contact us by phone or email, or interact with us on WhatsApp, and how we use, store, and protect it.

02Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to respond to your enquiries and deliver our services:

  • Contact form submissions: Your name, company name, mobile number, state, and your message describing your machining problem or product enquiry.
  • WhatsApp communications: When you message us at +91 8660234766, we receive your mobile number and message contents via WhatsApp Business (Meta Platforms).
  • Email communications: When you email toolflux@gmail.com, we receive your email address, name (if provided), and message content.
  • Phone calls: We may note your phone number and relevant details discussed during a call.
  • Website usage data: Anonymised data about pages visited, session duration, and approximate location region may be collected via analytics tools. This data does not identify you personally.

We do not collect payment card details, government identification numbers, or any special category data under the Information Technology Act, 2000 or the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

03How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • Responding to product enquiries, application engineering requests, or technical support questions.
  • Providing quotations, tool recommendations, and dispatch confirmations for orders you place with us.
  • Sending follow-up information directly related to an enquiry you initiated with us.
  • Maintaining business records for accounting, taxation, and dispute resolution as required by Indian law.
  • Improving our website and calculator tools based on anonymised, aggregated usage patterns.

We will not send you unsolicited marketing messages. Any follow-up contact from TOOLFLUX will be in direct response to an enquiry you started.

04Sharing Your Data

TOOLFLUX does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: WhatsApp Business (Meta Platforms) for customer communication; Google (Gmail, Maps) for internal operations. These providers have their own privacy policies.
  • Legal obligation: We may disclose data if required by Indian law, a court order, or a lawful government authority.
  • Business transfer: If TOOLFLUX is acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the successor entity, which will be bound by an equivalent policy.

05Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use browser cookies or localStorage to store your calculator inputs or preferences during a session. These are functional-only and do not track you across other websites.

If we use a third-party analytics service such as Google Analytics, it may set cookies to collect anonymised traffic metrics (page views, referral sources, session duration). This data cannot identify you personally.

You may disable cookies via your browser settings at any time. Core website content remains accessible; some calculator preferences may not persist between sessions.

06Data Retention

We retain your contact information and enquiry records for as long as our business relationship is active, or for the minimum period required by Indian law. Under the Companies Act 2013 and GST regulations, financial records must be retained for at least six years.

WhatsApp and email histories are subject to those platforms' own retention policies.

If you request deletion of your data and no legal obligation requires us to retain it, we will delete it within 30 days.

07Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Restricting access to enquiry data to TOOLFLUX personnel who need it to serve you.
  • Serving our website exclusively over HTTPS (TLS encryption).
  • Using password-protected, two-factor-authenticated accounts for email and communication platforms.

No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, contact us immediately at toolflux@gmail.com.

08Your Rights

Under applicable Indian privacy law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (once in full effect), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your personal data, subject to our legal obligations.
  • Objection: Object to specific processing of your data.
  • Grievance redress: Lodge a complaint with us or, once operational, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any right, contact us at toolflux@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 8660234766. We will respond within 30 days.
