## THE ONE-PAGE GUIDE

Send as a message, or print it.

> **NANDI Med — quick guide**
>
> **Installing**
> Android: open the link in Chrome, tap ⋮, tap *Install app*.
> iPhone: open the link in **Safari** (not Chrome), tap Share, tap *Add to Home Screen*.
> Computer: open in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar.
>
> **Seeing a patient**
> 1. Tap the **+** button in the middle of the bottom bar.
> 2. Type the **phone number first**. If they have been before, their whole history appears.
> 3. Fill what you need. Nothing is compulsory except phone and name.
> 4. Tap **Save consultation**.
>
> **After saving**
> **Send advice on WhatsApp** — writes the message, you press send.
> **Print** — prints on your clinic letterhead.
>
> **What the patient sees**
> Medicines, timing, before/after food, your advice, tests, next visit date, and a link to upload their reports.
>
> **What stays private**
> Prescription / Dilution box. Consultation fee. Your notes.
>
> **The five tabs**
> Home — today at a glance.
> Patients — everyone you have seen, searchable.
> **+** — new consultation.
> Reminders — who is due for follow-up.
> Settings — clinic details, passcode, backup, replay the guide.
>
> **Set a passcode:** Settings → Passcode → Set a passcode.
> **Back up:** Settings → Export backup. Once a week is enough.
> **Watch the guide again:** Settings → App & help → Replay the tutorial.
>
> **If something breaks:** call me. Do not reinstall or clear your browser data, that erases the records on the phone.
